Upload a photo to generate your numbered color palette.

How to play

  1. Choose your photo.
  2. Pick detail and number of colors.
  3. Select a color from the palette.
  4. Tap squares with the matching number.
  5. Complete your picture and save it!
🎉 Congratulations! You finished your picture!
